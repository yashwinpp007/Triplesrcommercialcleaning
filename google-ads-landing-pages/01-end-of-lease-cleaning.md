## 10. FAQ

**H2:** Questions people ask before booking

**What does an end of lease clean include?**
A top-to-bottom clean of kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, windows and floors, including the oven and rangehood. The checklist above shows what we cover, and your quote lists what's included for your property.

**Will it pass the agent's inspection?**
We clean to the standard agents check, but the agent or landlord makes the final decision. If they raise a reasonable item within the scope of your clean, tell us promptly and we'll arrange a return visit.

**Can I get my full bond back?**
We can't promise that. The bond depends on the whole condition of the property, including damage and wear that cleaning can't fix. A thorough clean removes the most common reason for deductions.

**Do I need to be home?**
No. Many customers hand over the keys or arrange access with the agent. We'll agree the plan when you book.

**Do you bring your own equipment and products?**
Yes. We bring professional equipment and products.

**Do you clean carpets?**
Carpet steam cleaning is available as an add-on and is quoted separately.

**How far ahead should I book?**
As early as you can. Final weeks before a move are busy, so contact us once your vacate date is set.

**What if I only need part of the property cleaned?**
Tell us what you need. We can quote a smaller scope, but check your lease and the agent's requirements first.

**Do you service my suburb?**
We cover Greater Sydney. Enter your suburb in the form and we'll confirm.
