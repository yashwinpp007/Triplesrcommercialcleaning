## 10. FAQ

**How much does house cleaning cost?**
It depends on the size of your home, the number of bathrooms, how often you want us and how dirty it is. Send us your suburb, bedrooms and bathrooms and we'll give you a clear written quote.

**Do I need to be home?**
No. Many customers arrange access with a key, lockbox or building manager. We'll agree the plan before the first visit.

**Will the same cleaners come each time?**
We aim to keep the same team so they learn your home. If the roster changes, we'll tell you.

**Do you bring your own equipment and products?**
Yes. If you prefer specific products, such as fragrance-free, tell us and we'll work with them.

**Do you clean homes with pets or kids?**
Yes. Let us know about pets (for example, if a dog needs to be secured) and any products to avoid.

**What if I'm not happy with the clean?**
Tell us as soon as possible. We'll arrange a return visit for reasonable issues within the scope of the clean.

**Can I pause or change my schedule?**
Yes, with notice. Call or message us.

**Do you also clean apartments and townhouses?**
Yes, in Sydney, including buildings that need lift bookings or access arrangements.

**Do you do end of lease cleaning too?**
Yes. See our end of lease cleaning page, or ask us for a quote.
