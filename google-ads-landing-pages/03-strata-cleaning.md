## 11. FAQ

**How much does strata cleaning cost?**
It depends on building size, number of floors, car park, frequency and the scope. We inspect first, then send an itemised written quote you can take to your committee.

**Can you provide a Certificate of Currency?**

**Do you work with strata managers and building managers?**
Yes. We coordinate access, keys, fobs and any lift or loading dock bookings with the manager before we start.

**What days and times do you clean?**
Most strata cleans happen on weekdays during the day, and we can arrange early-morning or other times. We agree a schedule that causes minimal disruption to residents.

**Can you take over from our current cleaner?**
Yes. We inspect, write a new scope, agree a start date and handle the changeover.

**How do we know the work has been done?**
You get a written checklist, and we provide sign-off sheets on request. You also have a direct number to call.

**What if there's a problem between visits?**
Call 0427 724 809 or email us, and we'll respond as soon as we can. For reasonable issues within scope, we'll arrange a return visit.

**Do you clean car parks and bin rooms?**
Yes. Bin rooms and car parks are common strata scope. Sweeping is part of regular cleans and pressure washing is available as an add-on.

**Can you do a one-off deep clean before an AGM or sale period?**
Yes. We can quote a one-off deep clean or pressure wash.

**Do you also quote for window cleaning?**
Yes, as an add-on or separately.
